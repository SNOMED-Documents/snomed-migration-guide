# Coastal Regional Health Authority - Multi-year phased programme

**Organisation.** A regional health authority covering 25 provider organisations, approximately 1.2 million patients, three legacy EHR vendors, and a complex mix of clinical services. No single migration pattern suited the whole organisation.

**Approach.** An explicit phased hybrid, structured as a programme rather than a project.

- **Year 1:** Full data migration, system unchanged across all 25 organisations. Regional terminology server established. Legacy records SNOMED CT-coded. Regulatory conformance achieved. Clinicians see nothing change.
- **Year 2:** Partial system migration - new SNOMED CT primary-terminology interfaces for problem lists and allergy capture across the authority. Full clinical training programme delivered to all sites.
- **Year 3:** Expanded system migration. New interfaces for prescribing and results review in the organisations where clinical teams were ready. Others continue at reference terminology.
- **Year 4 and ongoing:** Continuous optimisation. Progressive shift of additional workflows from reference to primary terminology as clinicians build confidence and vendors deliver new features.

**Terminology strategy.** Progressive shift from reference to primary across workflows and organisations.

**System and data migration.** Phased hybrid across all four cells of the system-data matrix over the programme lifetime.

**Outcomes.** Regulatory conformance across all 25 organisations by end of Year 1. Measurable improvement in decision-support quality across the authority by end of Year 2. No single organisation asked to absorb more change than it could manage in any one year.

**Lessons.** Sustained programme management across multiple years is the critical competence. The annual budget conversation was the most difficult governance challenge - the programme needed protected multi-year funding, and the benefits of later phases depended on the infrastructure built in earlier ones. A programme board with clinical, technical, and finance representation met quarterly and maintained visibility of both progress and forward dependency across all 25 organisations.

**Typical profile.** 3–5 years. Tens of millions across the programme, spread across multiple annual budgets and provider organisations.
