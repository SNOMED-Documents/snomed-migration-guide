# Meridian Regional Medical Centre - Greenfield, full modernisation

**Organisation.** A new-build regional hospital opening in 2027. 400 beds, all-new clinical services, no legacy EHR or historical data.

**Approach.** Full FHIR-native infrastructure from day one. Every workflow - problem list, allergy management, prescribing, orders, results, discharge summaries - designed around SNOMED CT primary terminology at the point of capture. Data migration was trivial (no historical records to convert). System migration was extensive - every clinical workflow was new.

**Terminology strategy.** Primary terminology throughout.

**System and data migration.** Full system migration. Every clinician joining Meridian underwent two-day SNOMED CT familiarisation before accessing the system. No legacy workflow existed to fall back on.

**Outcomes.** A fully SNOMED CT-native clinical record from day one of operation. FHIR exchange with regional partners from go-live. Full semantic search across all clinical data from the first patient record.

**Lessons.** Greenfield implementations are the most straightforward technically and the most demanding in terms of clinical change management - there is no legacy alternative for clinicians who find the new system difficult. Training and ongoing support were required at a higher intensity than anticipated in the project plan. The two-day familiarisation was necessary but not sufficient; structured at-elbow support in the first two weeks of clinical operation made the difference between confident adoption and early resistance.

**Typical profile.** 2–4 years of build for a greenfield hospital of this size. An existing organisation attempting the equivalent as a rip-and-replace should plan 4–6 years.
