# Case Studies

The following case studies illustrate the range of approaches organisations take to SNOMED CT migration. All are composite organisations drawn from patterns observed across real migrations; they are representative rather than records of individual projects.

- [Riverside Community Hospital](riverside-community-hospital.md) — Starter migration, allergy data
- [Central Regional Health Board](central-regional-health-board.md) — Reference terminology at scale
- [Northern Federation of Family Practices](northern-federation-of-family-practices.md) — Primary terminology in primary care
- [Meridian Regional Medical Centre](meridian-regional-medical-centre.md) — Greenfield, full modernisation
- [St Aidan's General Hospital](st-aidans-general-hospital.md) — Partial system migration, full data conversion
- [Kimberley Primary Care Group](kimberley-primary-care-group.md) — Minimum viable migration
- [Coastal Regional Health Authority](coastal-regional-health-authority.md) — Multi-year phased programme
