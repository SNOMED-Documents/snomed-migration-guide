# Northern Federation of Family Practices - Primary terminology in primary care

**Organisation.** A federation of 30 GP practices sharing a modern SNOMED CT-enabled EHR. The EHR vendor had built strong SNOMED CT search-and-select into the problem list and allergy capture modules. Clinical leadership was supportive of change.

**Approach.** Primary terminology for problem list and allergy capture; reference terminology for prescribing and lab review. The federation's SNOMED CT journey centred on clinical training and change management more than technical implementation - the EHR already had the capabilities; what was needed was a structured programme to develop clinician confidence.

**System and data migration.** Partial system migration (new primary-terminology workflows for problem list and allergies) combined with full data migration (historical problem lists and allergy records converted to SNOMED CT).

**Outcomes.** Problem-list coding granularity improved significantly: conditions that had been recorded as broad ICD-10 categories were now recorded as specific SNOMED CT concepts. Drug-allergy decision support improved in direct proportion. Clinicians who completed the training programme reported that SNOMED CT search was faster than the previous interface once they had used it for two to three weeks.

**Lessons.** The limiting factor was clinical training capacity, not technology. Dedicating a trained super-user in each practice - a GP or nurse with additional SNOMED CT support - dramatically accelerated adoption in their site compared with practices that relied on centrally provided remote training alone.
